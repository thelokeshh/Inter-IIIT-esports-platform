# IEC — Inter-IIIT Esports Championship Platform
Product case study of the Inter-IIIT Esports Championship platform, covering product strategy, requirements, registration workflows, team management, admin operations, leaderboards, and future roadmap.

**Product Management · Product Strategy · Platform Operations**

## Overview

The Inter-IIIT Esports Championship (IEC) platform was created to provide a centralized digital destination for students across IIITs to discover participating colleges, register teams, access championship information, and follow leaderboard updates.

Beyond the public-facing website, the product included an administrative system for managing college requests, users, teams, staff permissions, and game-specific leaderboard updates.

## The Problem

Inter-college esports participation lacked a centralized platform for students and organizers to access consistent information and manage the process across multiple IIITs.

The challenge was to create a reusable digital system that could support future editions rather than relying entirely on scattered communications and manual coordination.

## My Role

**Founder & Event Advisor — Product Ownership**

I led product-level decisions and managed the website development process, working with developers and the organizing team to translate operational needs into platform requirements.

My responsibilities included:

- Defining product requirements and website features.
- Planning registration and college-approval workflows.
- Structuring team and player management.
- Defining leaderboard and match-result update workflows.
- Organizing administrative responsibilities across different people and game functions.
- Coordinating developers, content contributors, and operational admins.
- Identifying improvements for future editions.

*The website was implemented by developers; my contribution focused on product decisions, requirements, coordination, and management.*

## Product Features

- **College directory:** Centralized information about participating IIITs and their coordinators.
- **College approvals:** Admin workflows for reviewing and managing college requests.
- **Team management:** A centralized directory for registered teams and their associated colleges and games.
- **Leaderboard management:** Admin-managed team statistics, standings, stage labels, and match-result updates.
- **Role-based administration:** Separate staff and admin responsibilities for distributed operations.
- **Public information platform:** Championship details, game information, participating colleges, and updates.

## Product Operations

The platform used a distributed administrative model. Different admins managed registrations, team information, and game-specific leaderboard updates. This divided the workload while keeping information accessible through one platform.

Leaderboard information was updated manually because matches took place on external gaming platforms rather than inside the website.

## Impact

- 22 participating IIITs.
- 10,000+ website visitors/users, as reported by the project.
- 100,000+ Instagram impressions/reach, as reported by the project.
- A reusable platform foundation intended to support future editions.

*Metrics should be accompanied by analytics screenshots or other supporting evidence where available.*

## Future Roadmap

- Improve registration status visibility and onboarding.
- Reduce manual leaderboard updates where reliable integrations are possible.
- Strengthen data validation and admin permissions.
- Improve analytics and reporting for organizers.
- Make the platform easier to reuse across future editions.
